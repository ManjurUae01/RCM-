# Elite Workforce Solutions – Private Lead CRM

A professional private CRM system for managing UAE manpower and workforce business prospects.

## Purpose

Elite Workforce Solutions helps manage potential companies and manpower opportunities for:

- Electricians
- Electrical Technicians
- Mechanical Fitters
- Welders
- HVAC Technicians
- Maintenance Workers
- Skilled Manpower

## CRM Features

- Lead Dashboard
- Add New Lead
- Edit and Delete Leads
- Lead Pipeline
- Company & Location Management
- Industry & Trade Tracking
- Lead Status
- Priority Management
- Follow-up Date
- Last Contacted Date
- Next Action
- Activity History
- Search & Filters
- WhatsApp Contact
- CSV Import
- CSV Export
- CSV Template
- Mobile-Friendly Interface

## Lead Pipeline

Prospect → Contacted → Discussion → Requirement Confirmed → Quotation → Deal

## Target Areas

- JAFZA / Dubai
- Dubai South
- Sharjah
- Hamriyah Free Zone
- Abu Dhabi
- Mussafah
- KEZAD
- Ras Al Khaimah

## Important

Company records are treated as **prospects unless a manpower requirement has been directly confirmed**.

Public-source company information should be verified before contacting or treating a company as an active requirement.
